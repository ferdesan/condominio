# ADR-001: A Detail Route for the Closed Statement

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-09-18

## Context

The statement lives as the fourth section of `/financeiro`, with no route of its
own — the decision recorded in `balancete-mensal` ADR-008, which weighed
linkability against keeping the document beside the ledgers it summarises and
chose the ledgers.

Adding the individual movements changes what the screen is. A summary of six
category lines sits comfortably beside three other sections; a document that also
lists every payment and every paid expense of the month is the thing a síndico
opens, reads, prints and sends. The section would grow past the point where the
other three sections remain reachable, and the reason to keep them adjacent —
checking a line against the ledger — is served just as well by a link back.

The repository is not starting from a blank rule. `frontend-cruds` ADR-004 is
titled *"Dialog-Based Create and Edit, With a Detail Route Only for
Condominiums"*, and it granted that one route on an explicit criterion:

> The condominium summary is reference material a síndico returns to, which is
> what a page is for.

A rendered account is reference material by definition.

## Decision

The closed statement gains a route of its own, `/financeiro/balancete/:mes`,
guarded by `financial-closing:read`.

The section in `/financeiro` keeps the summary it has today and gains a link to
the full document. Nothing is removed from it.

The route is **not** a navigation entry: it is reached from the section, and from
a link someone pasted. That follows `/perfil`, which is already a route outside
the menu, tested by its own case rather than by the `REGISTERED` list
(`routes.test.tsx:344`). The invariant that compares registered routes with
`NAV_ITEMS.length` keeps meaning exactly what it means.

The route works for an open month too, computed live and labelled as such. A
document that only exists after the month is closed would be unreachable during
the month it describes, which is when the síndico is actually working.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Expand the category line inside the section

- **Description**: clicking a category reveals its movements in place.
- **Pros**: no route, no new screen; the summary stays in view; prints with the
  section.
- **Cons**: the document is still not linkable, and the section — already the
  longest screen in the frontend — grows with every expanded line.
- **Why rejected**: it solves the reading and not the sending. The request was a
  screen, and the thing being built is a document people pass around.

### Alternative 2: A dialog per category

- **Description**: the movements of a category open in a modal.
- **Pros**: the established pattern for detail in this codebase (ADR-004); keeps
  the page short.
- **Cons**: a modal closes on a stray click, cannot be printed, and cannot be
  linked. ADR-004 itself rejected a dialog for the condominium summary on the
  first of those grounds.
- **Why rejected**: the same argument that gave condominiums a page gives one
  here, with more force.

### Alternative 3: A menu item for the statement

- **Description**: `/balancete` as a twenty-sixth navigation entry.
- **Pros**: discoverable without going through Financeiro.
- **Cons**: separates the document from the ledgers permanently, and adds a menu
  entry for a screen opened once a month.
- **Why rejected**: `balancete-mensal` ADR-008 already weighed and rejected this;
  nothing in the detail changes that reasoning.

## Consequences

### Positive

- A closed month has a URL. It can be bookmarked, pasted into a message, and
  reopened months later.
- The section stays short: summary plus a link, with the long table elsewhere.
- Printing has a natural home — the whole route is the document.

### Negative

- Two screens now show the same month, and a change to the summary has to be
  made in both or deliberately in one.
- The route is the second in the system reachable only by link. A reader who
  never opens Financeiro will not discover it.

### Risks

- `routes.test.tsx` renders routes through `serveEmptyWorld`, whose `apiGet`
  throws `ApiError 422` for any URL not listed in `AUXILIARY_READS`. A case for
  this route means the closing URLs must be added there — the first entries
  since the statement was built, and a deliberate departure from
  `balancete-mensal` task_04, which kept that list untouched because
  `/financeiro` does not read the statement on mount.

## Implementation Notes

The route is registered under its own `ProtectedRoute permission="financial-closing:read"`,
and not under the `charge:read` that guards `/financeiro`. A role that reads
charges without reading the rendered account should not reach the document by
typing its address.
