# ADR-008: A Fourth Section Under /financeiro, Not a Menu Item of Its Own

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-09-18

## Context

The statement needs somewhere to live in a navigation that already has 25
screens. The Financeiro screen is the one place in this frontend that holds
several resources under a single route: Cobranças, Despesas and Plano de contas
are three sections switched by local state, with no route of their own
(`financial-page.tsx:45`, `:118-152`), a shape argued in the tier-3 TechSpec on
the grounds that the resources are read together.

The statement is read together with all three: it *is* those three, summed.

The mechanics of each option are known. A fourth section is an entry in
`SECTIONS` (`financial-labels.ts:67-73`) and a branch in the ternary. A new menu
item is an entry in `NAV_SECTIONS`, a `<Route>`, an addition to `IMPLEMENTED`
(`app-router.tsx:40-66`) and a new row in the hand-written `REGISTERED` list of
`src/test/routes.test.tsx:140-167`, whose `toHaveLength(NAV_ITEMS.length)` fails
until all of them agree.

## Decision

The statement is the fourth section of `/financeiro`, switched by the same local
state as the other three, with no route and no menu item.

Two consequences are deliberate and are design rules, not accidents:

- **The section's data hook is called inside the section component**, never at
  page level. The section mounts only when selected, so the statement is not
  read when `/financeiro` opens. The boot surface of the route is unchanged,
  which is why `AUXILIARY_READS` in `src/test/routes.test.tsx:71-101` needs no
  new entry.
- **`serveFinancial` does need the new URLs.** It throws on any URL it does not
  recognise (`test-utils.ts:202-223`), so the closing endpoints are added there
  even though no existing case opens the section.

The route guard stays `charge:read` (`app-router.tsx:186-188`); the section
checks `financial-closing:read` for itself, exactly as Despesas and Plano de
contas already check their own permissions, and hides itself from the switcher
when the reader lacks it.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: A `/balancete` route and a menu item

- **Description**: a twenty-sixth screen, in the Operação section of the menu.
- **Pros**: linkable and bookmarkable; printable without first navigating to
  another section; the reader finds it by scanning the menu.
- **Cons**: separates the statement from the three ledgers it summarises, so
  checking a line means leaving the screen and coming back; and it makes the
  menu longer for a screen most roles open once a month.
- **Why rejected**: the same argument that put four financial resources under
  one item applies with more force to the one screen that reads all of them.
  Linkability is a real loss and is accepted.

### Alternative 2: A section with its own URL state (`?secao=balancete`)

- **Description**: keep the section, add a query parameter, as the LGPD screen
  does with `?tab=` (`lgpd-page.tsx:37-53`).
- **Pros**: linkable without a new route or menu item; the precedent exists in
  this codebase.
- **Cons**: the Financeiro screen deliberately keeps section state out of the
  URL, with the reason written at `financial-page.tsx:111-117`; adding it for
  one section would make the other three behave differently from the fourth.
- **Why rejected**: inconsistency inside one screen costs more than the link is
  worth. If URL state is ever wanted here, it should arrive for all four
  sections at once.

## Consequences

### Positive

- No change to `navigation.ts`, `app-router.tsx` or the `REGISTERED` list, so
  the route test keeps passing without edits and keeps meaning what it meant.
- The reader can check a statement line against Cobranças or Despesas by
  switching sections, without losing the month in the process.

### Negative

- The statement cannot be linked or bookmarked, and a browser reload returns to
  Cobranças. Printing requires two clicks from a cold start.
- The Financeiro screen grows to four sections and roughly 1,700 lines across
  its section files. It is the largest feature directory in the frontend and
  this makes it larger.

### Risks

- A section hidden by permission changes the switcher's contents per role, so a
  test that indexes the switcher positionally would become role-dependent.
  Mitigation: cases select the section by its accessible name, as
  `openSection(label)` in `financial-page.test.tsx:59-78` already does.

## Implementation Notes

The section id is `closing`, and its label is the word the assembly uses:
"Balancete".
