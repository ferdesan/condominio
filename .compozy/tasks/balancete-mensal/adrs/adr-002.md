# ADR-002: Opening Balance on the Condominium, Inherited Through Closings

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-09-18

## Context

A statement that does not open with the previous balance does not close. Nothing
in the model holds a balance: there is no bank account entity, no ledger, and no
cash position anywhere in `backend/src/modules/financial/`. The four financial
entities record movements, never a standing figure.

The plan offered three paths (`docs/balancete-mensal-plano.md`, "2. De onde vem
o saldo anterior?") and the product owner chose the first two together: a
starting balance typed once per condominium, and from then on each closed month
hands its final balance to the next.

## Decision

Two columns on `condominiums`:

- `opening_balance` `DECIMAL(12,2) NOT NULL DEFAULT 0`
- `opening_balance_date` `DATE NULL` — the cutoff the balance refers to

And the opening balance of month M resolves in this order:

1. If M-1 has a **closed** closing, its `closing_balance` is the opening balance
   of M. The statement says so in words: "inherited from the closing of
   ago/2026".
2. Otherwise the opening balance is `condominium.opening_balance` plus every
   cash movement between `opening_balance_date` and the first instant of M. The
   statement says "calculated from the opening balance of 01/01/2026".

Path 2 is not a fallback for errors; it is how the first months work before
anything has ever been closed, and how a month reads after its predecessor is
reopened.

`opening_balance_date` null means there is no lower bound: everything the
database holds is counted. That is the honest reading of "no cutoff was given".

The two fields are edited on the **Condomínios** screen, through the existing
`PATCH /condominiums/:id`, under `condominium:update`. The statement displays
the opening balance and its provenance; it never writes it.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Opening balance only, no inheritance

- **Description**: every month is computed from the cutoff forward.
- **Pros**: one field, no closing record needed for the arithmetic to work.
- **Cons**: a retroactive movement silently changes every subsequent month, and
  reprinting March in September gives a different number with nothing to explain
  why.
- **Why rejected**: this is precisely the failure the closing concept exists to
  prevent.

### Alternative 2: Account/cash entity with ledger entries

- **Description**: a real account, real entries, real reconciliation.
- **Pros**: correct; makes bank reconciliation possible.
- **Cons**: an entire module — entry, counter-entry, reconciliation, transfers
  between accounts — and every existing payment and expense would need to
  project into it.
- **Why rejected**: out of proportion to a first statement. Nothing in this
  decision blocks it later: an account module would replace the resolution rule
  above and keep the closing record as is.

### Alternative 3: Opening balance stored on the first closing

- **Description**: no condominium column; the first close asks for the balance.
- **Pros**: one fewer column; the balance lives where it is used.
- **Cons**: the number is unreachable until someone closes a month, so the
  statement is wrong — not unavailable, wrong — for every month before the first
  close.
- **Why rejected**: the statement has to be right the first time it is opened,
  before anyone has closed anything.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Reading a closed month is a lookup, and reading an open month is a
  computation, with the same shape either way.
- The provenance line makes the number auditable by a human: every balance says
  where it came from.
- `charge_due_day` and `syndic_term_ends_at` already establish the precedent for
  per-condominium financial configuration and a cutoff date typed as `date`.

### Negative

- The permission to set the opening balance is `condominium:update`, not
  `financial-closing:manage`. A treasurer who can close months cannot set the
  starting balance unless they can also edit the condominium.
- Path 2 is O(history): it sums every movement since the cutoff. At the scale
  this product addresses (tens of condominiums, hundreds of units) this is two
  indexed aggregate queries, but it is not free and it grows.

### Risks

- An opening balance typed with the comma swallowed by `input type="number"`
  would be wrong by a factor of ten and look plausible. Mitigation: the field is
  `type="text"` with `inputMode="decimal"`, per the rule already recorded for
  this repository; see "Known Risks" in `_techspec.md`.

## Implementation Notes

The migration that adds the columns is the same one that creates
`financial_closings` (ADR-005) and patches the seeded roles (ADR-007) — one
migration, one deployment step.
